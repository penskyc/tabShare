Guitar tabs · multiplayer

tabShare

Open a tab. Invite the band. Everyone sees the same score, hears the same mix, and plays along together.

Log in in your browser Windows app .exe Mac app .dmg · Apple Silicon

Rooms are invite-only: the host creates a room and shares the link and password.

1

Same score.
Same moment.

Everyone in the room sees the same tab, live. Jam sync keeps playback, speed and the whole mix together, so when you hit play, the band hears it with you.

  • 0Live cursors, selections and playheads · per person, in their colour
  • 3Jam sync · play, pause, seek, speed, tracks, mute/solo, volume
  • 5Shared loops · select a passage, loop it for the room
  • 7Synth or the original · a YouTube recording, tap-synced to the bars
2

Any song,
one search away.

Search Songsterr from the library and add a song for the whole room in a second, with every track. Or drop in your own files, or write a tab right in the app.

  • 0Songsterr search and import · all tracks, named
  • 2Guitar Pro 3–8, MusicXML, Capella · drag and drop
  • 4alphaTex editor · write tabs as text
  • 5Per-track view, mute, solo and volume
3

Learn it
on the neck.

A fretboard under the tab shows any scale or arpeggio in any tuning, or follows a track and lights up the notes it plays as the song goes. Tune up without leaving the room.

  • 0Follow a track · its notes light up under the playhead, in its own tuning
  • 2Scales and arpeggios · 14 scales, any root, note names or intervals
  • 3Guitar and bass · 4- to 7-string tunings, left-handed, up to 24 frets
  • 5Chromatic tuner · listens to your rig's dry input, or your mic
  • 7Zoom the tab · 50–200 %, remembered on each device
4

Plug in.
Sound like you.

Bring your guitar or bass into the room through a neural amp model, a cab IR and your favourite plugins, then send that tone straight into the call.

  • 0NAM amp models · local .nam files or browse TONE3000
  • 2Cab impulse responses
  • 3VST3 plugins · before or after the amp, on/off switch, search, their own windows (desktop app)
  • 5Video and voice call · pop out split screen, picture-in-picture, pick a screen to share, "original sound" for instruments
  • 7Chat · with link previews
5

The desktop app
gets out of the way.

Same rooms, same site, plus a native audio engine. Your interface runs through ASIO on Windows and Core Audio on Mac, so you hear yourself without the browser's delay. It also hosts your VST3 plugins, and it updates itself.
